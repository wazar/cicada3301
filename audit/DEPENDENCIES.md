The usable chain is now: frozen rune-only input → full reference-group arithmetic
controls → bounded synthetic detector controls → measurements with explicit
boundaries. It does **not** extend automatically to a common cipher across pages,
an English plaintext, a particular filter, or an OTP-class conclusion.

A's dataset/page map v1 are immutable. D parses raw input independently and compares
all arrays/source positions; its original-page labels depend on A's map. The fresh
reviewer separately checks parsing/counts but does not make every image join an
independent visual adjudication. B's expected answers pre-exist its implementation,
but come from community reference outputs with related transcription ancestry.

The proposed first experiment depends on actual structured-key plants and a
calibration of the entire frozen candidate set. PARALLEL-01's random-key controls
do not satisfy those additional gates. See
[proposal](parallel-01/coordination/PROPOSED-EXPERIMENT.md); it has not run.
