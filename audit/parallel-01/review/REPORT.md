## Independence, controls and limits

A and D use separately written parsing implementations but the same raw transcription and alphabet. D obtains physical page labels from A, so their agreement cannot be promoted to independent image-map verification. A's map is complete at filename level but physical region inventory and image transcription are partial. Its archive-byte check supports identity against a retrieved manifest, not cryptographic attribution or steganography absence. B's complete group comparisons are sound within downloaded transcriptions; physical LP1 page cuts, covers and disputed loss-of-divinity glyph remain unverified. Three related community transcriptions are not three independent witnesses.

C's final selection reproduces the production two-page acceptance formula, including its narrower200-shuffle null. Its additional wrong-key and paired-shuffle panels test the full selection choices they advertise. Negatives use one fixed ciphertext per length; positives reuse two fixed text windows. These are conditional key-distribution replicates, not a broad language sample. Structured controls are not independent Bernoulli trials. The report states these qualifications and does not extrapolate to billion-trial calibration. It honestly distinguishes a correct key, exact rune recovery and a score flag. R19 pair controls and stored R21 arithmetic cannot validate absent I2 models or all later settings. The procedural eight-candidate blind test had shared-filesystem access, which is disclosed.

D's convolution lower bound is valid under the stated independence and plaintext increment law, and bounds expectation rather than every observed count. Its counterexamples validly refute unqualified flat-IoC→full-key, repeat-deficit→unique-rejection-loop and no-boundary-repeat→continuous-key implications. They do not constitute an alternative explanation of LP2. Corpus floors and historical period thresholds are not reproduced; section-level statistics are explicitly missing. Retain that partial status.
