## One proposed next experiment — not executed

**Blind full-field transcription against the shared-error alternative.** Freeze these256 coordinate sites, but show a fresh reader shuffled, unlabeled two-glyph crops with adequate top/bottom margin and no existing transcript, byte values, or allowed-alphabet restriction. Mix in a fixed20 known-text control glyph pairs containing0/O/o, I/l/L/i/j, S/s, W/w, including ambiguous-height examples rendered from a separately documented reference; calibrate the reader on the controls before unblinding. Require20/20 control pairs exact; otherwise stop without judging puzzle disagreements. Then reveal the fixed page/row order and compare all256 readings with v1. A disagreement must be preserved with both readings and the original wider row, rather than resolved through a decoder score. Any independently corroborated disagreement distinguishes “the common text is exact” from “a shared-source glyph error survives”; complete agreement strengthens the text input but does not validate base60 or any cipher hypothesis. This bounded input experiment targets the specific inherited control gap instead of repeating already executed payload transforms.

The controls' font/reference must be documented and their glyph distinctions demonstrably applicable before claiming a quantified image accuracy rate. Until such a blind check, this version is a coordinate-linked, visually corroborated reconciliation—not certified ground truth.
