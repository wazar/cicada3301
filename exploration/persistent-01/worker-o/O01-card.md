# O01 — instruction heads predict operand counts

Two competing mechanisms considered: (A) one visible rune encodes a one/two-letter plaintext token under a fixed deterministic dictionary; (B) explicit-delimiter units are instructions, with their first rune specifying an operand-count class. Mechanism A with deterministic word-reset segmentation inherits repeated-word preservation already tested by F09; arbitrary context-dependent tokenization makes no fixed new prediction without extra specification. We therefore execute B's distinct structural prediction, rather than another compression parameter trial.

Assumption challenged: each explicit unit consists entirely of homogeneous encrypted letters. Alternative: first rune is an opcode and remaining runes operands. Predict held unit length from first rune. Unit length bins1..7,>=8; first rune29classes. No semantic naming of opcodes. Train firstfloor(2m/3) units of each discovery page, predict final units with a pooled Dirichlet-smoothed head/length distribution. Baseline uses training length distribution; page-specific training length distribution rescales global opcode likelihoods, so mere between-page length shifts are not treated as an opcode signal. Laplace1 fixed throughout, no fitting/tuning alternatives. Statistic held mean log likelihood gain over page baseline. All per-page predictions retained.

Complete-procedure null999: independently circularly shift each page's first-rune sequence relative to its length sequence and rerun every fit/prediction. Lengths, head-symbol counts, cyclic head-order dependence, page geometry retained. Assumes exchangeability of head-sequence phase relative to lengths; not a universal natural-language null. Training/test are experiment-local split of previously examined discovery data, not pristine global holdouts. No separator physical-class interpretation needed.

Controls4: actual page/unit count and exact unit-length schedules; fixed seeded random partition of29opcode symbols into8nonempty arity classes, choose first rune from corresponding length class; remaining original unit runes retained. Controls contain changed head/body boundary repeats and do not claim full ciphertext distribution matching. Each99complete nulls. A fifth weak/no-signal fixture uses uniformly random heads with same lengths (descriptive, not a false-positive-rate estimate). Require all4strong controls positive gain and tail<=.05 before interpreting real negative. Real tail<=.01 and positive gain would warrant review, not identify grammar: ordinary lexical prefixes or variable-length token encoding can also predict lengths.
