# Q04 — correcting the objective does not recover unknown maps at the frozen budget

The joint LM-plus-emission optimizer failed to recover all eight planted codebooks: four preserved held-source controls and four new samples generated by the exact frozen model. None of the64 retained restart maps equals its planted full map. No real-page or conditional-null search was run because these controls do not justify one.

There are two distinct limitations. In the two longer model-generated controls, truth scores above every map the optimizer found, yet it remains unrecovered: this finite search misses a better solution. In the two shorter model-generated controls, adaptively searched alternatives score above truth. Thus N06's fixed-candidate ranking success does not transfer to unknown-map optimization; its eight old competitors did not represent the new search's competitors. A finite sample under a correct generator need not make the planted codebook the likelihood maximum, especially without a codebook prior. These outcomes do not uniquely isolate finite-sample ambiguity, optimization geometry or prior mismatch.

The held-source controls preserve P05's source-model limitation. Their original ciphertexts, plaintexts, truth maps and search seeds were copied exactly from control-{group}-0. Correcting the optimization objective changes found maps, but their continuing poor recovery cannot be rescued by asserting that the correct score must rank truth first. Paired original accuracies and all eight alternative outputs are retained in results.json; oracle best-among-eight accuracies are diagnostic only and never select the reported mapping.

## Frozen method and new coverage

The only substantive optimizer change from P05 is adding the exact one-shot0.83 emission log probability with coefficient1. The alphabet remains the same17 consonants, and every29-rune map remains surjective. The same frozen source model, initialization, swap/reassignment proposal family, total-score temperature3→0.05, eight starts and5,000 nominal proposals per start were used. There is no added bin-allocation prior, reweighted term, extra restart, alternate alphabet or language model.

The objective recomputes the full prefix after every valid proposal. Bin sizes include all29 rune assignments, including unused runes. Initially, outside the previous mapped bin or for a singleton, output probability is1/k. An eligible same-rune repeat has probability0.17/k; another member in that bin has probability1/k+0.83/[k(k−1)]. LM first two symbols use independent unigrams; later symbols use the frozen conditional trigram table. Only first floor(2n/3) observations influence optimization or selection. Suffixes are scored conditionally after selection and are not globally pristine puzzle holdouts.

Four new model controls have lengths324/195/54/62 and the same four planted maps. Their source seeds470400+2g and emission seeds+1, all uniform draws, initial emissions, rejection coins and redraws are saved. Truth is never an argument to the optimizer: its search process receives only model.txt, ciphertext/cut and a seed. Truth maps are used separately in score-only arithmetic checks and post-search evaluation. All per-position LM/emission terms, prefix/suffix totals, maps, decoded strings, wrong positions, unused runes, ranks and tie checks are retained.

P05D rescored old alternatives without searching; N06 supplied truth among nine fixed candidates. Q04 actually searches unknown maps under the corrected joint objective. This is the concrete new coverage; it does not broaden the prior conclusions into a cipher-family exclusion.

## Validation and accounting

The new scalar C++ kernel was inspected locally and by review17 before compilation. Its score-only interface agrees with independent Python on72 packet/map cases. Review17 additionally checks974 independent objective fixtures,12 suffix perturbations and an instrumented finite proposal trace, including an unused-rune reassignment whose emission changes without changing decoded text. The annealer is an optimizer, not a posterior sampler; no stationary sampling claim is made.

Exactly320,000 nominal proposals were attempted across eight packets and64 restarts;265,198 were valid,22,814 accepted. All eight best maps per packet are preserved. First two packets were the frozen cost pilot; the remaining six ran unchanged. Compile logged0.372s, preparation0.092s, arithmetic checks0.440s, two-packet pilot0.085s and remaining six0.147s. These are logger wall durations, not CPU-work estimates. No repeated search, expansion or real-page output was generated after the control failures.

Independent review17 also replays all1,296 generator uniforms, all64 restart maps and10,160 per-position scores, exact inherited source packets, counts, ranks and recovery metrics. Its full control audit passes; no optimizer rerun was required. All old P05/P05D/N06 files remain unchanged. The current directory holds exact input snapshots and hashes, new generator draws, source code, raw process outputs, complete results and a publication manifest; the compiled executable is excluded from that manifest. No reserved pages/images, external services, Git writes or background process.

Decision: stop this frozen unknown-map search family at its measured control boundary. More starts or a different LM are not silently queued. A future approach must state a materially different inferential mechanism and demonstrate unknown-map recovery before real-page rankings can support an exclusion. The overall mission remains with the coordinator and the original deadline.
