Normalize each retained glyph rectangle to24x48 grayscale; fit discovery-even hierarchical complete-link shape classes at RMSE<=0.16. Hit must pass existing4px separation /20% minority /50% heldout improvement gates and occur within at least three classes, with clean visual agreement of glyph identities. Plant +8px width on randomly half the actual source crops, measure resized occupied bounding widths with frozen original class assignments. One fixed clustering pass; if shape grouping merges distinct glyphs, classify result as instrument-limited rather than retuning.
