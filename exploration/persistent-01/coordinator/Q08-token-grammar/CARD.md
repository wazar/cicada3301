Exact mechanism: a finite dictionary of 29 distinct nonempty strings, built from k<29 distinct one-character base symbols by at least one binary concatenation merge, retaining both children as tokens. Encoding restarts at each explicit word and always emits the longest dictionary prefix. Every dictionary token maps bijectively to one rune, shared across admitted pages. Therefore the already measured complete nonself graph is a direct contradiction under every bijection. No permutation search is required.
