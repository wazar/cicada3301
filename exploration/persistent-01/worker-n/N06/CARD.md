# N06 frozen model-matched discrimination

Before simulation, freeze 100 independent samples for each of the 16 existing P05 control truth maps, at that control's original length (324,195,54,62). Each packet keeps its original eight saved alternatives, including any duplicates. No new key/map fitting, source model training, real-page run, or selected-source substitution is permitted.

Generate plaintext from the exact frozen P05 source distribution: first two symbols independent unigram, then its conditional trigram table. Generate ciphertext under that packet's truth map: uniformly choose a member of the plaintext bin; if this equals the previous ciphertext rune and the bin has another member, redraw uniformly among other members with probability .83. Bin members are sorted by rune ID; this has the same distribution as the original ordered bins. Separate local Python RNG seeds for source and emission are 1706000+1000*packet_index+2*rep and that number+1. Packet index is 4*group+original_control_rep; simulation rep is 0..99. Save every uniform draw, plaintext, ciphertext, map and score.

Use original cut floor(2*n/3). For each of nine fixed maps (truth first, then eight original alternatives), sum exact source log likelihood plus exact one-shot emission log likelihood. Report prefix, conditional suffix and full totals separately. Suffix scores retain preceding context, and involve no fitting. Prefix selection is among these nine only; evaluate its suffix margin against truth. Also rank suffix independently as a diagnostic, not a selection procedure for real data.

Ranks count strictly greater scores with absolute tolerance 1e-9; save all numeric ties and exact map duplicates, including observed-prefix/suffix map-equivalence. Primary endpoint is truth attaining the highest prefix joint likelihood; distinguish strict winner from tied top. Secondary endpoints: truth suffix/full rank, truth-minus-best-alternative margins, prefix-selected map suffix margin, and pairwise truth-minus-alternative means. Break down each original codebook and original length/source group; do not count 1,600 dependent-in-model samples as independent evidence about natural language or unknown-map search. Conditional on each codebook/model, RNG samples are independent.

This tests likelihood discrimination against eight previously optimized but now frozen competitors on fresh model-generated data. Good discrimination would support a source-distribution mismatch explanation for the original held-text failure, while neither proving it uniquely nor eliminating finite-sample ambiguity over the enormous unsearched mapping family. Failure would show ambiguity or weak discrimination even under the specified model at these lengths. No claims about generic homophonic ciphertext or original LP plaintext follow.

Run one CPU, logged <=900 seconds. Preserve inputs under snapshots, raw results under gzip, summaries and source/emission random draws. Stop after the fixed 1,600 samples; no parameter adjustment or extra length after outcomes.
