# M20: H19 continuation follow-up — no predictive candidate

Training on originals 0/1 and selecting only phase on page2's prefix produced suffix gain **−0.1022060781**, empirical tail **0.381** (380/999 fullprocedure nulls at least as large). This does not support this common-matrix, stable-coordinate-distribution continuity hypothesis across the three physical pages. The page2 suffix was unused in this experiment's fitting/selection; all are previously examined discovery pages, not pristine global holdouts. No chapter identity is claimed.

Six actual-size planted controls all detected at 0.005 (199 nulls each); all recovered the three page phases, while only three recovered all three projective rows. Row recoveries were 2,3,2,3,2,3. Statistical detection and full key-direction recovery are different outcomes. Controls used 262/266/201 rune geometries, distinct/disjoint solved-source spans across training and test, varied matrix scales, affine shifts and phases. Exact inverse/re-encryption passed in all six. These six related English source controls are a limited power panel, not a universal guarantee for every register or key.

The chosen training phases were [2,0], matrix [[1,18,16],[1,23,9],[1,24,15]], and page2 phase1 selected on its 33 prefix blocks; 33 independent suffix blocks were scored. Alternative page2 prefix/suffix gains were phase0 −0.151337/−0.027323, phase1 −0.133862/−0.102206, phase2 −0.136243/−0.062319. These alternatives are retained, not post-selected by suffix.

Bounds: 871 projective directions, nine training phase pairs, three test phases; a common fixed 3x3 GF29 block inverse up to coordinate relabeling, no skip/rejection transitions; stable nonuniform coordinate categorical register rather than an English scorer. Uniform/compressed sources, changing marginals, per-page matrices, arbitrary block sizes and more elaborate transforms are not tested. The memory1 null models expected repeats and fitted unigram weights, not exact histograms or higher-order dependence. Scalar multiplication and additive output shifts preserve the detector statistic, not semantic key identity.

Actual execution: 2,200 complete searches (six controls +1,194 control nulls + one real +999 real nulls), 19,800 train phase comparisons /17,245,800 direction-validation values and 6,600 held-page phase comparisons, 16.81 logged seconds.
