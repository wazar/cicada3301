J02 changes mechanism from adaptive TTF to one-output-rune-per-input disjoint homophonic substitution, allowing arbitrary homophone choice and arbitrary transposition. The resulting same-symbol collision count is a rigorous lower bound for every such codebook/choice sequence. No English score or partition search is involved.

Sampling distributions are assumptions. The finite-count inequality itself needs none about homophone RNG, plaintext order or cipher transposition; extrapolating from solved source frequencies to unsolved text does. Missing spellings, source encodings with merged rune distinctions, fewer source symbols, within-page codebook switches and polygraphic systems remain outside coverage.
