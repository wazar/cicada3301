## Limits and next decisions

Page-edge units may be textual continuations; P02 tests the source-defined withinpage representation as given. Exact length copies can miss one omitted separator, numeral/non-rune unit changes, inserts, deletions, cumulative-coordinate templates, or templates crossing page boundaries. Multiset permutations remove local linguistic length dependence, so an extreme future hit would still need stronger controls and a frozen prediction.

Next executable alternatives:
1. A single-deletion synchronization test on these same pairs: permit exactly one inserted/deleted complete unit, maximize aligned exact units with an explicit span-size penalty and rerun the full null. Plant the same10-unit templates with one deleted unit to quantify the added sensitivity and false positives. This changes alignment, not keys or scorer settings.
2. A boundary continuity test across the six genuinely adjacent page boundaries: freeze prefix/suffix unit-length comparisons across neighboring pages versus matched withinpage cut locations, preserving explicit separator types. This asks whether page breaks are merely physical cuts rather than whether independent pages duplicate a template. Do not join across reserved4 or9.

Neither is implied by the present negative. Parent allocation should select the next discriminating use of effort; P01/P02 provide no candidate justifying reserve release.
