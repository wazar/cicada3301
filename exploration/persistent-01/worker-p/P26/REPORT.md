For each proposed cycle, nonzero step counts receive0.5 smoothing. The cycle maximizing training likelihood is fixed before held evaluation; held pages do not choose it. The baseline fits conditional probabilities w_j/(1−w_i), j!=i, by the prescribed convex conditional likelihood, not empirical marginal weights.
