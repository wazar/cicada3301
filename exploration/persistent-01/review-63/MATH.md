# Review63 preliminary independent mathematics

Originally written pending the frozen Q12 card and exact seed convention. Update: Q12 uses the first-k literal seed convention described below. Its free errors are exactly the negative seed entries, and the final period entry is their seed sum. Full numerical review subsequently confirmed this convention and all enumerated seed scores; see REPORT.md. The following original reasoning preceded those checks.

For a fixed ciphertext and decoder recurrence p_i = c_i − sum(p_(i−1), …, p_(i−k)) modulo29, let p and q be two trajectories using different initial conditions, and e_i=p_i−q_i. Wherever the recurrence applies, e_i=−sum(e_(i−1), …, e_(i−k)). Every block of k+1 consecutive errors therefore sums to zero. Subtracting consecutive zero-sum blocks gives e_i=e_(i−k−1). Once initialized, error is periodic with period dividing k+1; it does not necessarily decay or vanish.

Conversely, every length-(k+1) error pattern with zero sum satisfies the homogeneous recurrence. Choosing any k of its values freely in GF29 fixes the last. Thus there are exactly29^k distinct initial-condition trajectories, provided the specified seed convention parametrizes those free values bijectively. k2/3/4 give841+24,389+707,281=732,511 nominal hypotheses per packet. Periods smaller than k+1 and zero errors create aliases across the three k families; nominal cells are not necessarily distinct plaintexts.

If the first k keystream entries are literal seed symbols, then p_i=c_i−seed_i for i<k; reconstruction of the actual seed follows from those first k plaintext values. If instead seeds specify prior plaintext history, the first k outputs are related by a different invertible recurrence map. These conventions must be checked separately from the periodic-error identity. The chosen zero-initialization base also determines the shift coordinates, but not their completeness when correctly parametrized.

A trigram language score with explicit fixed boundary tokens depends locally on at most three consecutive rune values. Boundary tokens do not consume recurrence positions. Under p_i=base_i+e_(i mod(k+1)), each score contribution can be grouped by rune phase into a function of at most three consecutive phase shifts; boundary contributions may depend on fewer. Initial language-model context and trailing boundary must be included exactly. Summing such tables under the single zero-sum constraint can enumerate the whole seed family exactly. Table construction, phase wrap, arbitrary seed reconstruction and top-candidate alias accounting remain to be independently checked against scalar recurrence, not inferred from this proof.
