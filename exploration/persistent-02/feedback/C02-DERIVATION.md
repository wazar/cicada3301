# Zero-sum offset optimization

All arithmetic in this paragraph is modulo29. Two decodes with seeds s and s' obey e_i=P'_i−P_i=−sum(e_{i−k},...,e_{i−1}) after the seed region. Subtract the same relation at i−1 to get e_i=e_{i−k−1}. Thus the first k errors determine e_k=−sum(first k errors), then a zero-sum period k+1 repeats. A nonzero seed error persists; it does not disappear. Conversely any zero-sum period determines a unique seed relative to the zero-seed baseline, s_j=−e_j for j<k.

With fixed explicit boundaries, each P03 score increment references at most the current and two preceding runes. Boundary tokens are constants and sometimes make a factor ignore one or two of its arguments. Collect increments by current rune phase, producing W_j(e_{j−2},e_{j−1},e_j). The total unnormalized score is their cyclic sum, including initial context constants already incorporated into W. This remains true for periods>=3, including the k2 case where the three phases are distinct.

Let m=k+1 and fix e0=a,e1=b. Closing score after all remaining choices is U_m(u,v)=W_0(u,v,a)+W_1(v,a,b). For j=m−1,...,2 define U_j(u,v)=max_x[W_j(u,v,x)+U_(j+1)(v,x)]. It is the best remaining cyclic score when the sum constraint is ignored. Therefore accumulated score+U_j(last2) bounds every feasible completion above. This relaxation preserves all trigram factors and cyclic closure; only sum(e)=0 is absent.

The optimizer expands prefixes in descending bound order. At one remaining variable the modular sum fixes it. At two remaining variables only29 possibilities remain. Completed assignments are feasible lower bounds. Any unexpanded frontier node keeps its upper bound. Nodes skipped by the1e−10 pruning tolerance also contribute to a retained maximum discarded bound. Termination with frontier exhausted thus carries an explicit numerical tolerance rather than pretending exact-real arithmetic. A cap leaves best feasible value, frontier/discarded upper bound and their gap; no capped result is declared exact without a vanishing gap.

Floats are deterministic float64 under the recorded platform, not interval arithmetic. The derivation proves bounds in exact arithmetic; implementation tests assess rounding against independent direct scores and exhaustive small-k maxima. Tolerances are stated openly. Remaining ties and global n-best paths are not exhaustively enumerated: alternatives are complete feasible solutions encountered during incumbent construction/search.

Cost is 841 conditioned initial pairs times roughly m*29^3 operations for suffix tables, plus data-dependent branch expansion. It avoids blind29^k enumeration but offers no polynomial worst-case guarantee. Table memory is (m+1)*841*29^2*8bytes (~57MB at k8). Initial deterministic feasible paths and suffix construction are uninterruptible inside solve; branch-loop time checks and the external command timeout are distinct limits.
