# C09 — no distributed common-period signal

The preregistered shared-period aggregate has conditional family rank **.67**. Its maximum standardized value is 1.28813 at k7. The tested k values were 2–12, with 45/43/42/42/40/39/38/37/32/27/26 eligible page features respectively. This is a new summary of the existing C07 arrays, with **zero new seed or page coverage**.

C07 asked whether one page/period was exceptional. C09 asked whether individually weaker evidence accumulated across pages sharing the same uninterrupted recurrence period. Both are ordinary under their specified comparisons. The latter therefore supplies no rationale to fit a selected seed or widen the period range.

All twelve distributed natural-language capability books produced rank .005 and selected their true k. These use four previously frozen source excerpts and planted k2/5/8; overlapping slices and repeated authors make them correlated capability examples. Three uniform-plaintext books produced ranks .07/.565/.695. This does not establish detection power for every language, deliberately flattened plaintext, different periods per section, reset streams or interruptions.

The comparator preserves each page's first rune and adjacent-equality mask, while sampling other runes uniformly. It does not preserve F positions, histograms or interpage structure. Equal page weights, supported period groups, pooled standardization and the across-period maximum were frozen before computing actual aggregate scores. Reuse of discovery inputs and adaptive selection of this additional analysis mean .67 is an exploratory conditional rank, not a programme-wide probability.
