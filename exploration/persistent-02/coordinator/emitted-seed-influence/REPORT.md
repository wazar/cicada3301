# Fixed-mask seed influence

C11 changes a mathematically consequential assumption. Once seed consumption is complete, normal transitions update the k-rune coefficient history by an invertible companion map, while an emitted literal zero can reduce its rank. Rank zero means future plaintext under the same future cipher and interruption decisions no longer depends on the initial seed. Before seed completion, unseen seed coordinates can re-enter; history rank alone is insufficient.

This is different from C05's normal-only history, where literal F leaves feedback history unchanged and completed history retains full seed rank. The result does not extend Q12's uninterrupted periodic-error identity to C11.
