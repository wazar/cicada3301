# Shared-seed reset factorization

Let k≥2 and m=k+1. Within each segment, the firstk plaintext symbols are c_j−s_j; later symbols are c_j−Σ(lastkplaintext), all modulo29. Subtract the zero-seed recurrence q. The differences start x_j=−s_j for0≤j<k, then x_k=Σs_j. Subtracting neighboring homogeneous recurrence equations gives d_j=d_(j−m). Therefore every segment shares the same m-periodic offsets with Σx=0, restarted at its first rune. This argument also covers a segment shorter than k.

Scoring uses the same interpolated trigram tables as the frozen source models, with explicit separator token29, initial context(29,29), and no scoring-context reset. Within a segment, any emitted rune/separator factor depends on at most three neighboring phases and belongs to W_j[x_(j−2),x_(j−1),x_j]. A reset is required to follow an explicit separator. Its first rune factor is L(previousrune,29,newrune), a pair between previousphase r and phase0. Accumulate it into B_r[x0,xr]. The next rune has context(separator,phase0), hence the ordinary W_1 factor. No other factor crosses the reset. When r=0 or1 the same storage remains valid; evaluation uses the diagonal or the conditioned pair respectively.

Fix x0=a,x1=b. Every B_j[a,x_j] becomes unary. At cyclic closure the terminal score is W_0[x_(m−2),x_(m−1),a]+W_1[x_(m−1),a,b]+B_0[a,a]+B_1[a,b]. The backward suffix Viterbi recurrence adds W_j[u,v,x]+B_j[a,x] and maximizes over x. It drops only the modular total constraint, so its suffix bound is admissible over exact real arithmetic. Branches retain the modular total; the final variable is forced, or the last pair is enumerated over29 choices. Interrupted heap nodes, children pruned within tolerance, and an interrupted popped frontier node retain upper bounds. All reported numerical claims use float64 and1e-10 tolerance, not directed-rounding certification.

A shared offset yields seed s_j=−x_j modulo29. Prefix fitting retains this exact seed for the full recurrence, including subsequent fixed reset marks. Continuation total is fulltotal−prefixtotal: this preserves the two scoring-context tokens crossing the cut and counts each explicit separator on its physical side. There is no seed fit or score-context reset at the continuation cut.
